# Гермодвери (Hermodoors)

Soviet blast doors for abandoned stations, for **NeoForge 1.21.1**.

They do not open by hand. Whoever places a door fills in its technical passport and picks which steps of the opening procedure it needs. Anyone who wants through later has to get through those steps on the door's control panel.

## The doors

| Block | Size | How it moves |
|---|---|---|
| **Гермодверь ДЗГ** (shelter blast door) | 2 × 3 | Swings out on its hinges (left or right) |
| **Гермозатвор** (metro blast gate) | 4 × 4 | Slides sideways into the wall (left or right) |

Both are unbreakable in survival, immune to explosions, withers and the dragon, and can't be pushed by pistons. A shut door also blocks light.

## Opening procedure

Right-click a locked door to open its control cabinet. The plate on the left lists the steps this door needs; a lamp shows the current one.

1. **Подача питания (power).** Close the main knife switch, then close the breakers. A breaker may only be closed after the breaker that feeds it; trace that on the pencil wiring diagram glued inside the cabinet. Close one out of turn and the whole panel trips.
2. **Давление (hydraulics).** Work the hand-pump lever to bring the manometer needle into the green band, then hold it there until the time relay runs out. The system leaks, and overdoing it lifts the relief valve. There's a bleed valve if you overshoot.
3. **Клиновые затворы (wedge clamps).** The seized clamp lever rocks back and forth. Click to strike with the sledgehammer when it lines up with the chalk mark. A clean blow loosens it; a miss jams it tighter and makes it rock faster.
4. **Штурвал (handwheel).** Grab the rim and turn it anticlockwise for the required number of turns. Rush it, especially through the tight spots, and your grip slips.

Hover over any control for an explanation in your language. The panel engravings are in Russian on purpose.

Once every step is done the seal hisses and the door opens. **It stays unlocked**: from then on anyone can open and close it by right-clicking (slowly, it's heavy), and a redstone signal works it too.

## The passport

* It opens by itself when you place a door: tick the steps, circle a difficulty category (I to III) and the hinge or slide side, then stamp it in the **М.П.** circle.
* Sneak + right-click with an empty hand reopens it. Only the person who placed the door, creative players and operators can do that (configurable). Re-stamping relocks the door and resets its procedure.
* **Списать изделие** (write the door off) dismantles the door and hands it back.
* A passport with no steps ticked makes an ordinary, very heavy door.

## Multiplayer

* Everything is checked on the server. A client can't skip a step: completions are checked against the puzzle (e.g. the breaker order) and against the fastest time a human could manage.
* One person works a door's panel at a time; others get "%s is already working on this door".
* People nearby hear the work (breakers, hammer blows, the relief valve) and see the clamps come loose, the handwheel turn and the signal lamps change.
* Progress on the clamps is kept if you step away. The handwheel springs back, and the pressure leaks away.

## Compatibility

* No mixins and no coremods; standard NeoForge registries, menus and payloads only.
* Recipes use common `c:` tags, so iron from other mods works.
* Tagged `c:relocation_not_supported` and `create:non_movable`, so contraption and relocation mods leave the doors alone; machines and fake players can't run a locked panel but can open unlocked doors.
* Works with Sodium/Embeddium/Iris style renderers: the moving leaf is an ordinary block entity renderer.

## Recipes

```
ДЗГ door:        Гермозатвор:
B I B            B P B
I D I            R Z R
B I B            B B B
```
`B` iron block, `I` iron ingot, `D` iron door, `P` piston, `R` redstone dust, `Z` ДЗГ door.

## Config

`serverconfig/hermodoors-server.toml` (synced to clients):

* `defaults.power/pump/clamps/wheel`, `defaults.difficulty`: what a freshly placed door starts with.
* `rules.ownerOnlyPassport`, `rules.redstoneOpensUnlocked`, `rules.enforceMinimumTimes`.

`config/hermodoors-client.toml`: `screenShake`, `typewriterJitter`.

## Building

Requires JDK 21.

```
./gradlew build              # jar ends up in build/libs/
./gradlew runClient          # dev client; runClient2 starts a second one for multiplayer tests
./gradlew runServer
./gradlew runGameTestServer  # server-side tests: placement, removal, unlocking, puzzle generation
```

Every push runs the same build and game tests on GitHub Actions and uploads the jar as an artifact.

## Art

Textures, the 5-pixel Cyrillic stencil font, block models and the synthesised sounds are generated by the scripts in `tools/artgen` (Python 3 with Pillow and NumPy, plus ffmpeg with libvorbis):

```
python3 tools/artgen/build_all.py            # everything
python3 tools/artgen/build_all.py --no-sounds
```
